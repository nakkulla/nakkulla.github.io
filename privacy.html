Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Hermes Assistant is used only by its owner. With the owner's consent it reads Google Calendar data from the owner's own Google account.

  • Calendar data is used only to show the owner their own schedule.
  • Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
